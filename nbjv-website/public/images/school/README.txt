=======================================================
NATUN BAZAR JATIYA VIDYALAYA — SCHOOL PHOTOGRAPHS FOLDER
=======================================================

To display photos from your Facebook page on the website:

1. Right-click any photo on your Facebook page and select "Save image as..."
2. Save the pictures into this directory:
   d:\Project\Natun Bazar Jatiya Vidyalaya Website\nbjv-website\public\images\school\

Recommended file names:
- photo-1.jpg   (Campus / Main Gate / Exterior)
- photo-2.jpg   (Classroom & Students)
- photo-3.jpg   (Sports & Activities)
- photo-4.jpg   (Events & Celebrations)
- photo-5.jpg   (Faculty & Teachers)
- photo-6.jpg   (Annual Day / Special function)

The website will automatically load and display your real school pictures
across the Homepage, Gallery, and Hero Slider with zero manual code changes!
